Our Story

Crafting hand-poured candles, organic bath teas, and custom monogrammed keepsakes for fine celebrations worldwide.

The Vision

Gifting elegance should be accessible for every wedding

Aura Favors began with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between luxury bespoke gifting and accessible minimum order quantities. We believe couples shouldn't have to choose between custom monogrammed elegance and low order minimums.

We partner directly with certified organic botanical suppliers, keeping our ingredients pure and eco-conscious. From hand-pressed wax seal stamping to custom silk ribbons, we work as an extension of your wedding planning team, delivering keepsakes built to delight.
