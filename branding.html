Bespoke Branding Services

From precision metal die-cast stamps to hot foil debossing, our atelier elevates every celebration favor into an enduring heirloom keepsake.

Custom Wax Seal Engraving

Solid brass stamps individually laser-engraved with your monogram initials, heraldic crest, or botanical vines. Each seal is hand-poured with flexible European wax that survives transit without chipping.

28mm Brass Head · Custom Flexible Wax

Metallic Hot Foil Stamping

High-precision heat-pressed foil debossing in 24k Florentine Gold, Matte Champagne, Rose Copper, or Satin Silver, applied with microscopic clarity across rigid boxes and deckled cotton tags.

Precision Hot Foil · 4 Metallic Finishes

Heraldic Crest Design

Hand-drawn intertwining letterform cyphers, floral botanicals, and bespoke family crests hand-illustrated by our studio calligraphers and delivered in vector and print-ready formats.

Vector Master Assets · Print & Web

Custom Scent Profiling

Signature olfactory formulation blending pure French lavender, golden amber, sandalwood, and neroli blossom oils to capture the romantic spirit of your ceremony and venue.

100% Essential Oils · Bespoke Blend

Plant-Dyed Silk Ribbons

Luxury Packaging Architecture

Engineered layer by layer to provide guests with an unforgettable, multi-sensory unboxing ritual.

Couture Packaging Anatomy

Each gift box is assembled by hand in our California atelier, ensuring seamless corners, velvet-touch textures, and perfectly draped ribbons.

Layer 01 — FSC Rigid Keepsake Box

1200gsm heavyweight board wrapped in Belgian bookcloth linen with hidden magnetic snap closures.

Layer 02 — Plant-Dyed Mulberry Silk

Hand-torn 100% natural silk ribbon draping the exterior with romantic, delicate feathered edges.

Layer 03 — Deckled Letterpress Hangtag

Heavyweight 600gsm Italian cotton paper with feathered deckled edges and hot foil stamped typography.

Layer 04 — Hand-Poured Seal Keepsake

Each wax seal is hand-melted and stamped by studio artisans, creating an artisanal, one-of-a-kind seal finish.
